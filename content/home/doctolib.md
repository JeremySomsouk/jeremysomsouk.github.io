Building the technical foundations of Doctolib's digital health strategy for patients.

Continuous preventive care: developing the patient "Care Plan", a health suggestion and reminder engine for consultations, screenings and examinations based on public health authority recommendations. The goal is to make Doctolib a trusted health advisor, able to proactively plan preventive care so patients do not have to remember it themselves.

Parental support: designing onboarding journeys and baby development tracking experiences, with personalized content, smart notifications and daily activities.
